# Vanguard Arm — Funding Playbook (the evidence ladder)

*Saved 2026-07-07. Companion to PLAN.md §11. Owner: Krithin (background workstream).
The model: each tier of proof unlocks the next tier of money. Don't cold-ask above your
current evidence tier — sweep the tier you're on, prep the next.*

## Current evidence in hand
Public repo (demo GIFs, textbook, first working skill) · competition timeline · IRC
registration upcoming. **Advisor + grants: in progress (background).** Mech spec delivered.

## Tier 1 — entitlements & near-automatic (~90–100%) — DO ANYTIME, ~1 hour total
| Source | What's needed | Get |
|---|---|---|
| GitHub Education | Student/club signup | Pro, Copilot, credits |
| MathWorks student competition program | Registered team + request form (rover comps officially sponsored — ERC on their list) | Licenses, training, mentors |

| Small cloud credits (AWS Educate class) | Student email | $100–300 credits |

## Tier 2 — vendor in-kind (30–50% now; higher after SDDR selection ~Dec)
- Camera/hardware vendors (RealSense-class, Luxonis…): hardware for logos + footage.
- **Action item (2 hrs, once): the sponsor one-pager/deck** — repo GIFs, timeline, team,
  ask tiers (livery/jersey/base-station branding, recruiting access, demo footage).
  **Have it ready THE DAY SDDR selection lands** — leverage spikes, every other team starts
  writing theirs that week.

## Tier 3 — institutional grants (GATED ON FACULTY ADVISOR)
| Source | Needs | Odds | When to file |
|---|---|---|---|
| NVIDIA Academic Grant | Faculty PI + proposal; our Isaac/GR00T VLA-field story is exactly their showcase profile | 25–40% w/ P1 on arXiv | After advisor + P1 (~Jan) |
| ISRO RESPOND | Faculty PI, BITS sponsored-research office routing, months-long cycle; PLAN §8 ≈ 80% of the proposal | 20–30%, payoff = lakhs/multi-year | After P1 (~Jan) |
| AWS/GCP research credits ($1–5k) | Short proposal + faculty affiliation | 30–50% | After advisor |

## Tier 4 — cash (GATED ON IRC RESULT, ~Feb 2027)
- Indian space/robotics startups (Skyroot/Agnikul/Dhruva/Pixxel/ideaForge class): recruiting +
  branding pitch; 40–60% for ≥1 meaningful sponsor **after** a finals run, low/awkward before.
  IRC's BPP assessment forces building this deck anyway — double-counted effort.
- CSR (Companies Act §135): route via university, relationship lead-time months — start warm
  intros post-IRC.
- BITSAA / alumni crowdfunding: story-driven; time campaign to competition news cycle
  (post-selection or post-result, never pre-season).
- IEEE RAS travel grants: per accepted paper, student-targeted — apply the day P1 is accepted.

## Realistic 12-month bottom line
- In-kind: sweep nearly everything applied for (₹1–3L value, near-certain).
- Cash pre-IRC-result: modest (₹0.5–2L, alumni/college channels).
- Post-P1 + IRC finals: RESPOND/NVIDIA/startup tier opens — one hit changes the season.

## The strategic line
**P1 (Dec) and the IRC result (Feb) ARE the fundraising campaign.** Every hour there is worth
ten on cold outreach. Only Tier 1 + the one-pager shouldn't wait for evidence.
